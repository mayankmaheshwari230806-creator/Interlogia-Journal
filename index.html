01 — ABOUT THE JOURNAL

Research begins with a question.
Understanding grows across disciplines.

INTERLOGIA is a proposed annual scholarly journal focused on the conversations between disciplines. It aims to provide a platform for original student and researcher work that examines questions from more than one intellectual perspective.

From the ethics of markets to the philosophy of public policy, from social transformation to the history of ideas, INTERLOGIA welcomes work that takes ideas seriously and builds its arguments carefully.

Clear standards.

Originality

06 — QUICK QUESTIONS

Before you contribute.

Who can submit to INTERLOGIA?

Students and researchers from colleges and universities across India are the intended audience for the inaugural call. Final eligibility criteria will be confirmed in the official author guidelines.

Does my paper have to cover both economics and philosophy?

Not necessarily. Papers may focus on one field, provided they fit the journal's scope. Interdisciplinary work is especially encouraged when the relationship between fields is clearly developed.

Is an ISSN already assigned?

No ISSN is stated on this draft website. The journal should display an ISSN only after it has been officially assigned.

Is publication guaranteed after submission?

No. Submission does not guarantee acceptance. The journal should publish its review and selection process before accepting manuscripts.
